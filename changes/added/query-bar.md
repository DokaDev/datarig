- A table tab has a query bar on the first line of its results: `WHERE` with each condition as a
  chip of the text that is sent, `ORDER BY`, `LIMIT`, how many columns are shown, and the rows of
  the result with its page arrows. A chip's `×` takes that condition out and runs the others as
  they were written; a click on another part opens the filter dialog there. `Space r w` puts the
  keys on the conditions (`x` takes one out), `Space r l` types the limit in the bar, `Space r c`
  shows the columns, `Space r r` counts the rows and `Space r A` opens the dialog. The bar says
  less as the window narrows but always marks what is applied, and it says when the last run
  failed, was cancelled or was refused.
