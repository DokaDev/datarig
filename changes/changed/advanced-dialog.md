- The filter dialog opens centred over the screen, the screen dimmed around it (on an 80 x 24
  terminal it still takes the whole screen).
- `Q` (or `SQL ▸`) in the filter dialog shows the SQL in its right pane, in place of the
  column's conditions: first the statement as it is sent, read only; again, the `WHERE` and
  `ORDER BY` texts to edit; again, it closes. The footer keeps its three rows; the condition
  chips are hidden while the SQL is shown.
